Zelvero POS — Marketing Quick Reference

Companion to the Complete Product & Marketing Guide. Not a substitute for it.

What it is

Zelvero POS is a shop system for the counter and the back office: sales, stock, customers, suppliers, the cash drawer, and reports. It works in Arabic and English and takes US dollars and Lebanese pounds in the same sale.

One line. Zelvero POS records the sale, the stock, the drawer, and what customers still owe — in Arabic or English, in USD and LBP.

Who it is for

  • Neighborhood shops and mini-markets that scan items, watch expiry, and mix currencies.
  • Phone and accessories shops that need serial numbers and more than one price.
  • Wholesale counters that sell on credit and hold more than one order.
  • Owners who employ cashiers and do not want them to see cost or supplier bills.

It is not a restaurant, clinic, pharmacy, or hotel system.

Main benefits

  • A sale can update stock and the drawer together.
  • USD and LBP can be received in one payment. Change can be given in either currency, at the shop’s rate.
  • Credit sales and later payments stay on the customer.
  • Purchases can be unpaid or partly paid, and stock can be returned to the supplier.
  • Returns put items back and adjust debt or cash.
  • The owner chooses what each staff user can see. Cost and profit can be hidden.
  • Net sales, gross profit, expenses, and net profit are separate. Missing cost is not treated as zero.

  • Post: a cashier login that cannot see cost.

Advertising restrictions

  • Do not advertise Zelvero AI. It is internal testing only and is not a customer feature.
  • Do not advertise in-app feature updates or the platform admin tools.
  • Do not invent prices, plans, trial length, installation fees, or support hours. Those terms are not confirmed in the product guide.
  • Do not promise a one-click full backup. Lists can be exported from screens. A full browser backup is not provided.
  • Do not say the product is unhackable or 100 percent secure.
  • Do not call sales “profit.”
